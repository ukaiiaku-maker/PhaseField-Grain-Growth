The extended baseline resolves the scaling gate. Over the objective interval from 5% population loss to the available ensemble endpoint (mean grain count 190 to 68.4), direct radius-space regression gives `n=1.9150` with a realization-bootstrap 95% interval `[1.7079, 2.1503]`. The fixed `n=2` comparator changes `R²` by only `-3.21e-5`, while dense-output residual autocorrelation is 0.995; the tiny free-exponent fit improvement is therefore not treated as independent evidence against parabolic growth. Independent measures give `n=2.056` for the number-weighted mean radius and `n=2.009` for the perimeter radius. The median-radius exponent is less stable and is retained as a sensitivity result. This validates conventional ensemble coarsening while explicitly identifying the earlier `n≈1` interval as a transient.
