# Phase-1 interpretation addendum

The completed 900 K shear-transition campaign established robust finite-time
retardation and reproducible *local* mechanical arrest across the tested
`K_s=0.25/0.30` bracket. Its 10,000-step target/censor classification does not,
by itself, establish global or asymptotic grain-growth stagnation.

Phase 1 therefore uses the terms **slow/intermittent high-backstress regime**,
**local mechanical arrest**, **long waiting/release intervals**, and
**finite-time strong retardation** until substantially longer, larger-domain
trajectories determine whether a finite growth law emerges. The historical
transition report remains unchanged as provenance; this addendum governs the
interpretation of all new long-time reports.

The authorized experiment is single-temperature (900 K), single-seed (5101),
and common-initial-state. It measures kinetic regimes over grain-size dynamic
range rather than judging a calculation by whether it crosses a prescribed
grain-count threshold in a fixed number of steps. No temperature or additional
seed campaign is part of Phase 1.
